Ground Truth Credential — Verify

Check a content hash against published GTC disclosure manifests.

How to verify

1. Obtain the SHA-256 hash of the content file. On macOS: shasum -a 256 file.html. On Linux: sha256sum file.html.

2. Paste the hash above and click Verify.

3. If a matching manifest exists, you'll see the deployer's structured disclosures — the conflicts, relationships, and conditions under which the content was produced.
