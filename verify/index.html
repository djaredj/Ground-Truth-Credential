Verify a Ground Truth Credential

Paste a content hash to check it against published GTC disclosure manifests.

Generate with shasum -a 256 yourfile.txt or any SHA-256 tool.

The GTC mark on a piece of content means a deployer-side disclosure manifest exists for it. The manifest declares the structural conditions under which the content was produced — AI system used, provider relationships, author conflicts — in a verifiable, hashable format.


GTC is a companion to provider-side provenance (C2PA Content Credentials, AI watermarks). It fills the layer those systems don't cover: the deployer's structural conflicts and epistemic context.
